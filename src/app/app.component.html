About the studio

A small studio with a deliberate practice.

Atelier Horizon exists to give ambitious ideas a form people remember. Since 2017 we have helped 120+ founders and teams turn positioning, product and identity into one coherent story.

We stay deliberately small: three disciplines, one team, no hand-offs. The people you meet on the first call are the ones drawing, prototyping and shipping your brand.

Clarity before decoration

Every element earns its place. If it does not guide the eye, it goes.

Process over guesswork

Research, prototypes and honest critique — no lucky stabs in the dark.

Partners, not vendors

We embed with your team and care about the outcome, not the invoice.
